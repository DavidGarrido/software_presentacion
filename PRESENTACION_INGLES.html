Where is Villa de Leyva?

Location

  • Country: Colombia
  • Department: Boyacá
  • Distance from Bogotá: ~157 km
  • Distance from Tunja: ~40 km
  • Altitude: 2,149 m above sea level
  • Founded: June 12, 1572

How to get there

  • By bus from Bogotá (~3.5 hours)
  • By car via Highway 55
  • Nearest city: Tunja (45 min)

Declared a National Monument in 1954 — its colonial architecture has been perfectly preserved.

Villa de Leyva is older, smaller and quieter than most Colombian cities. Its streets are whiter and wider than typical colonial towns. The weather is cooler and drier than Bogotá.

Plaza Mayor — The Heart of Villa de Leyva

Description

  • Size: 14,000 m² — one of the largest in South America
  • Entirely paved with large cobblestones
  • Surrounded by white colonial buildings
  • Central fountain built in 1617
  • Faces the Parish Church (1608)

Why It Is Popular

  • It is a symbol of Colombian heritage
  • Host of the Star and Kite Festival
  • Surrounded by restaurants and craft shops
  • Perfect spot to enjoy the sunset
  • Featured in many films and TV series

"The Plaza Mayor is not just a square — it is a living museum where time seems to stop."
